    What is real in this prototype?
    • Works: voice typing (the browser’s speech recognition, where supported), simple keyword guidance in three languages, read-aloud (browser voices), text size, contrast, simple and calm settings saved on this device, the “Call 108” phone link.
    • Simulated: family check-ins, consent controls and sharing. Nothing is sent anywhere.
    • Not built: accounts, cloud storage, notifications, clinical AI. Guidance comes from word matching, not medical assessment, and ignores age, history and medicines.
